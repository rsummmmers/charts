Credit

Your reputation for paying back what you borrow

"Credit" has three different uses. In accounting, a credit is a right-side ledger entry (opposite of a debit). In everyday use, "I'll give you a $20 credit" means the seller owes you $20. In financial systems — the meaning used here — credit is your reputation for paying back, your permission to borrow. A credit rating or credit score measures this. Extending credit to someone means allowing them to take on debt. Credit and debt are always two sides of the same transaction: the lender extends credit, the borrower carries debt. Good credit means you can borrow more at lower cost. No credit means no access to debt at all.
