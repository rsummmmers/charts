Price

The number agreed on at a specific moment of exchange

Price is not value. Price is what someone agrees to pay at one specific moment — shaped by supply, urgency, bargaining power, and expectations, not just usefulness. The same tomato is $2.99 at a grocery store, $0 from a neighbor's garden, and $8.00 after a drought. The value (it feeds you) doesn't change. The price swings with context.
