Wealth

Stored capacity to meet needs — tangible and intangible

Wealth is stored-up capacity to meet needs — tangible and intangible. A home you own. A full pantry. Good health (your body's ability to work and enjoy life without crisis). Skills that can always regenerate income. Relationships with people who would show up for you. These are all real wealth, even though none appear in a bank account. You can have significant wealth with very little money, and significant money with very little wealth.
