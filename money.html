Money

A coordination tool that lets strangers trade at scale

Money is a coordination tool. It lets millions of strangers trade without having to directly swap goods — a doctor can get food without trading medicine for it, a farmer can get healthcare without trading tomatoes for it. Money solves that problem by being a widely accepted stand-in for value. What makes it unusual: it only works because everyone agrees it does. A pile of cash in a town where no one accepts it is worthless. The agreement is the money.
