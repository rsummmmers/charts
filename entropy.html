Thermodynamics

The physical rules that govern all energy — and why they point in one direction

Two laws govern every energy transaction in the universe. The First Law: energy is never created or destroyed — only changed in form. A car engine, a body digesting food, a power station: all take in concentrated energy and produce some work plus unavoidable heat. The Second Law: every real process increases total disorder. Heat never flows from cold to hot on its own. Mixed cream never un-mixes. Burned wood never re-forms. These rules apply to every economic process too — including the burning of fossil fuels, which are roughly 300 million years of accumulated solar energy converted by living organisms and compressed over geological time. Once burned, they cannot be unburned. Efficiency reduces waste; it does not change the direction of the door.
